Consumers and Community Members

If you are a person with lived experience of serious illness or caregiving, who resides in Australia, we invite you to get involved with Voices.

Sharing Your Experiences

Sharing your experiences can influence:

  • how research is conducted,
  • how care is delivered,
  • the priorities set within policy,
  • how illness is discussed and supported by the community, and
  • experiences at the end of life in the future.

If you are interested in sharing your lived experience with palliative care, and potentially joining Voices, please fill out an expression of interest here.
